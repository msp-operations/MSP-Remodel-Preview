A lot of the emails we have are outdated, which means surveys and updates don't reach everyone. That's where you come in — by sharing some basic info, you help us plan better activities, organize events, and keep the community connected.

Your data, your control

Here's exactly what we do, and don't do, with your information.

What we do
  • Use your info to send you relevant updates and newsletters
  • Create anonymous, aggregated statistics for the website, for example how many alumni live in each country
  • Organise events in cities where alumni are located
  • Connect you with other alumni if you both agree
  • Always ask before featuring you publicly
What we never do
  • Share your personal information with third parties
  • Display your name or details publicly without explicit consent
  • Sell or monetise your data in any way
  • Spam you, we send three or four emails per year at most
  • Make it difficult to opt out, just email us any time

Want the full details? Our privacy policy explains everything in plain language.
